Please never feel pressured to forgive me quickly. Your feelings are important, and you deserve the time and space you need.

I don't expect perfection from you. I don't want you to be someone else. I simply want us to grow, understand each other better and create something filled with trust, respect, kindness and happiness.

If I could ask for one thing, it would be the chance to show you that I truly understood my mistakes and that I'm willing to work on them.

I'm sorry for the moments I made you sad, for the words I wish I had never said, and for the times I failed to understand your heart.

You matter. Your feelings matter. And you deserve to be loved with respect.

I'm truly sorry, my muddu wifey. 🥺❤️

With all my sincerity,
Yours ❤️
